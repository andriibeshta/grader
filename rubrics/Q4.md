## Q4 — EU AI Act GPAI obligations, 2025–2028

**Date-sensitive status, reviewed 2026-11-22.** The Digital Omnibus on AI was agreed in May 2026 and formally approved in July 2026. It postponed the high-risk obligations but did **not** postpone the GPAI obligations, prohibitions, or AI-literacy duties. Do not use the pre-Omnibus high-risk dates as the only correct answer.

### Must-have facts

- AI Act entered into force **1 Aug 2024**.
- Prohibitions and AI-literacy obligations: **2 Feb 2025**.
- GPAI obligations (Art. 53: technical documentation, downstream information, copyright policy, training-data summary): **2 Aug 2025**.
- GPAI enforcement and penalties: **2 Aug 2026**.
- High-risk Annex III (stand-alone systems): **2 Dec 2027**, postponed from 2 Aug 2026.
- High-risk Annex I (AI embedded in regulated products): **2 Aug 2028**, postponed from 2 Aug 2027.
- Article 50 transparency/watermarking for AI-generated content: **2 Dec 2026**.
- National AI regulatory sandboxes: **2 Aug 2027**.
- Systemic-risk GPAI threshold (≥10^25 FLOP): model evaluation, adversarial testing, incident reporting, cybersecurity.
- Fines: up to €35M/7% for prohibited practices; up to €15M/3% for other obligations including GPAI; SME caps.
- Small-provider first steps: classify role, document model, copyright/training-data summary, technical documentation, assess systemic risk, EU representative if needed, monitor codes/guidance.

### Common errors

- Treating 2 Aug 2026 / 2 Aug 2027 as the current high-risk dates without acknowledging the Digital Omnibus postponement.
- Claiming the Omnibus postponed GPAI obligations, prohibitions, or AI-literacy duties; it did not.
- Confusing the Article 50 transparency date with the high-risk dates.
- Wrong fines, or confusing provider vs deployer.
- Stating the postponement without an official EU source, or presenting a blog-only claim as settled law.
