## Q1 — RTX 5090 vs 4090 vs 3090 for 4-bit 27B–32B local inference

Must-have facts:
- RTX 5090: 32 GB GDDR7, 512-bit, ~1,792 GB/s, 575 W.
- RTX 4090: 24 GB GDDR6X, 384-bit, ~1,008 GB/s, 450 W.
- RTX 3090: 24 GB GDDR6X, 384-bit, ~936 GB/s, 350 W.
- 5090 bandwidth ≈ +78% vs 4090, ≈ +91% vs 3090; 4090 only ≈ +7.7% vs 3090.
- 27B Q4 weights roughly 15–19 GB; 32B Q4 roughly 18–20 GB; 24 GB cards are tight for 32B plus context.
- Measured 27B Q4: roughly 75 tok/s (5090), 43 (4090), 39 (3090) at 4K.
- Measured 32B Q4: roughly 61/40/35 tok/s at 4K; lower at 16K.
- Real 5090 advantage is usually ~1.3–1.7× over 4090, not the full 1.78× bandwidth ratio.
- 4090 vs 3090 speed gap is small (~10–13%).
- 32 GB mainly buys 32B/long-context headroom; 24 GB needs KV quantization/offload.
- MTP/speculative decoding can inflate 5090 numbers; compare same engine.
Common errors: wrong VRAM/bandwidth; claiming 5090 is always 1.78× faster; ignoring context/KV; treating 4090 and 3090 as very different.
