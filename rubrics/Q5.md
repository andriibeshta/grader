## Q5 — Docker Compose → Kubernetes for 5-person team, 20 services

Must-have facts:
- Compose: single-host/multi-container orchestration; simple YAML; fast iteration; no built-in HA/scheduling/multi-node; good for small teams.
- Kubernetes: scheduling, self-healing, rolling updates, service discovery, secrets/config, RBAC, multi-node; significant operational complexity; managed K8s reduces control-plane burden but adds cost.
- 20 services/5 people: migration only justified by HA/multi-node/scale/compliance/team growth; otherwise stay Compose or use a simpler orchestrator.
- Consider managed K8s, k3s/RKE2, Nomad, Docker Swarm, or managed container service.
- Decision framework: availability, growth, deployment frequency, skills, cost, stateful workloads, lock-in.
- Recommendation should be conditional, not “Kubernetes always.”
Common errors: K8s always better; Compose cannot run production at all; ignoring ops cost; recommending self-hosted K8s for a 5-person team without caveats.
