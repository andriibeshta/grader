# Generic Research Rubric

No independent fact checklist is available for this paper.

Score factual accuracy only on:
- internal consistency,
- whether claims are supported by the paper's own cited sources,
- whether numbers and conclusions agree with each other.
Do not assume a claim is true merely because it is stated confidently. Do not use outside knowledge to reward a claim unless the paper cites a source for it.

Score coverage, critical analysis, source quality, structure, and actionability using the global rubric.
