# Blind grading policy

Grade only the supplied text. Do not infer the producing model, pipeline, or
filename. Apply the rubric consistently to every report.

## Rubric (0–10 each)

| Criterion | Weight | 10 means | Penalize |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Factual accuracy | 25% | Core facts and calculations correct; claims supported; no contradictions | Wrong numbers, wrong dates, internal contradictions, unsupported claims |
| Coverage | 20% | All parts of the question and required tradeoffs addressed | Missing major dimension, shallow treatment |
| Critical analysis | 20% | Distinguishes measured vs estimated; notes uncertainty, confounders, limitations, contrary evidence | Overconfident single-number answers, no caveats |
| Source quality & citations | 15% | Relevant, credible sources; citations support claims; good density | SEO/irrelevant sources, citation mismatch, unsupported paragraphs |
| Structure & readability | 10% | Logical sections, scannable, coherent | Dense wall of text, poor organization, repetition |
| Actionable conclusion | 10% | Clear recommendation/decision framework with tradeoffs | No conclusion, data dump, vague advice |

Overall = 0.25*accuracy + 0.20*coverage + 0.20*critical + 0.15*sources + 0.10*structure + 0.10*action.
